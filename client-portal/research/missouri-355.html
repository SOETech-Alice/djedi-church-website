1. Missouri § 355 — Nonprofit Corporation Act

Legal Authority: Missouri Revised Statutes, Chapter 355 (Missouri Nonprofit Corporation Act)

Missouri § 355 governs the formation, operation, and governance of nonprofit corporations in Missouri. For Djedi Church, this statute provides the legal framework for organizing as a nonprofit religious corporation with corporate protection and limited liability.

1.1 Articles of Incorporation Requirements

  • Must include religious purposes language — clearly state the organization is organized for religious purposes
  • Must designate a registered agent — a Missouri resident or registered agent service
  • Must list initial directors — minimum of one director
  • Must include dissolution provisions — specify how assets will be distributed upon dissolution (must be for religious/charitable purposes)

2. Missouri Tax Exemptions for Religious Organizations

2.1 Property Tax Exemption

Legal Authority: Mo. Rev. Stat. § 137.110

  • Religious organizations can apply for property tax exemption in Missouri
  • Must apply to the county assessor
  • Must use the property exclusively for religious purposes
  • Separate application required — this is NOT automatic

2.2 Sales Tax Exemption

Legal Authority: Mo. Rev. Stat. § 144.030

  • Religious organizations can purchase items tax-free in Missouri
  • Must provide a Missouri Sales Tax Exemption Certificate (Form 1746)
  • Must use the purchases for religious purposes
  • Documentation required for each purchase

2.3 State Income Tax

  • Missouri has a state income tax (4.225% for individuals)
  • Religious organizations that qualify as 501(c)(3) are exempt from Missouri state income tax
  • Must file Missouri Form 990 (state version) if gross receipts exceed $25,000

3. Missouri Charitable Registration

Legal Authority: Mo. Rev. Stat. § 407.450 et seq.

  • Charitable organizations soliciting donations in Missouri must register with the Missouri Attorney General
  • Churches are generally exempt from charitable registration requirements
  • However, if Djedi Church engages in significant charitable activities (food banks, shelters, etc.), registration may be required

4. Federal Tax-Exempt Status — 508(c)(1)(A)

Under IRC § 508(c)(1)(A), churches are automatically exempt from the requirement to apply for 501(c)(3) recognition. This means:

  • No Form 1023 filing required — exemption is automatic upon organizing as a church
  • Automatic 501(c)(3) status — the organization is treated as tax-exempt from inception
  • Limited IRS audit authority — the IRS must follow strict procedures under IRC § 7611
  • First Amendment protections — religious autonomy doctrine protects church governance decisions
