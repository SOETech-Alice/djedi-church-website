Executive Summary

Comprehensive business consultation for establishing Djedi Church as a legitimate spiritual organization bridging ancient Egyptian traditions with modern consciousness science. This document provides actionable guidance across seven key areas.

Key Recommendations

1. LEGAL: Register as a church under IRS Section 501(c)(3) with automatic tax-exempt status via 508(c)(1)(A) designation
2. DIGITAL FIRST: Start with virtual presence before establishing physical space to minimize costs and build community
3. COMMUNITY FOCUS: Build authentic engagement through consistent content and genuine relationships
4. FINANCIAL SUSTAINABILITY: Implement tiered membership and diverse revenue streams
5. TECHNOLOGY: Use scalable, church-specific tools (Planning Center, Circle.so, ConvertKit)
6. GROWTH: Follow phased approach over 36 months with measurable milestones

Section 1: Legal Structure & Registration

Recommended Entity Type: Church under IRS Section 501(c)(3) via 508(c)(1)(A)

Why this works for Djedi Church:

  • Automatic tax-exempt status without filing Form 1023
  • No annual Form 990 filing requirement
  • Special IRS audit protection
  • Tax-deductible donations
  • Property tax exemptions in most states

Registration Timeline: Weeks 1-4: Draft bylaws, select board, file incorporation. Weeks 5-8: State tax-exempt status, bank account, financial controls. Months 3-6: Federal filing (if needed), property tax exemption, insurance.

Cost Estimate: $1,500-6,000

Section 2: Physical Community Setup

Phased Approach (Recommended):

  • Phase 1 (Months 1-12): Virtual presence only
  • Phase 2 (Months 12-24): Shared spaces for gatherings
  • Phase 3 (Months 24-36): Dedicated physical location

Event Programming:

  • Weekly services: Sunday 10-12 AM (60-90 minutes)
  • Workshops: 2-4 per month ($25-75/person)
  • Retreats: Quarterly ($200-500/person)
  • Special events: Monthly (solstices, equinoxes, Egyptian holidays)

Cost Estimate: $6,000-24,000 annually for shared space; $24,000-96,000 for dedicated space
